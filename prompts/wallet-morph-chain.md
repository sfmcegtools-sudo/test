# Build "Wallet Morph Chain" — 7-second seamless-loop UI motion piece

## Brief (verbatim from the animator)

Build “Wallet Morph Chain” as a 7-second seamless-loop UI motion animation in HTML/CSS/JS/SVG, rendered with Chromium (Playwright) and FFmpeg. The piece is one continuous chain of morphs—not separate scenes—where a tiny blurred squircle becomes the Wallet app icon, dissolves into glowing dots that stretch into a Face ID glyph, a small black card grows into a tall Wallet card, morphs into a tilted 3D map card, flattens and draws a route from India to Dubai, then shrinks back into the starting squircle. Use a 1080×1080 canvas at 60 fps in LIGHT ONLY, with a white background and faint #F5F5F6 vignette, Inter font bundled locally at -0.02em tracking, and only the specified palette: Face ID blue #1E90FF, card black #000–#151515, stack colors #489CCB/#5CC24A/#F07566, progress green #22E83C, continents #BFBFBF, route blue #2F8CFF, origin ring #FFC83D, destination fill #EF5B55, glass rgba(70,70,70,0.55), and play button #0A84FF. The Wallet icon is a 340px light-gray outer squircle with a dark-gray inner squircle, cream pocket glyph, coral scalloped edge, four thin card edges, and a 36px semibold “Wallet” label; the Face ID glyph is 330px with 14px round-cap blue strokes, four corner brackets, two eye bars, J-shaped nose and smile arc, plus two glow layers. The Wallet card is 84% canvas width with 44px radius, cropped at the bottom, containing a 56px “Wallet” title, 68px white “+” button, money-bag SVG, “ 120,000” digit-by-digit, progress from 48% to 80%, and credit card; S4 3.80–5.00s: the front card’s geometry morphs into the 920×490 map card, radius changes from 44px to 36px, rotation becomes -8° with perspective, fill transitions black to white, stack cards sink and fade, the tilted blank card receives a heavy soft-gray halo and graticule, a yellow ring and India bubble appear, the graticule crossfades into continents, the card flattens and halo tightens, and the stats panel enters last from the lower-left; S5 5.00–6.30s: the camera pushes to approximately 2.3× on the midpoint between the two cities, the Dubai bubble pops in, UAE fills red, and a blue arc with glowing head draws from India to Dubai while the slider thumb travels left-to-right in sync, then holds; S6 6.30–7.00s: the map card shrinks, rotates, blurs and morphs back into the tiny blurred squircle matching the first frame exactly. Shapes must lead content by 80–150ms with 30–50ms staggers; every transition must reuse persistent geometry and interpolate position, size, radius, rotation, fill and blur rather than crossfading scenes; morph midpoints should peak at 14–24px blur with incoming content starting at the same blur, opacity may dip to approximately 0.55 but never reach a blank frame except at the loop point; blurred objects should emerge rotated about -10° to -14° and settle to 0° with a spring; floating objects should have a wide soft-gray halo that tightens on settlement. Use closed-form springs with emergence ω≈15 ζ≈0.72, card growth ω≈16 ζ≈0.82, map flatten ω≈14 ζ≈0.85, and label pop ω≈26 ζ≈0.70; entrances use cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1), route/camera use cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1), exits are roughly 40% faster, and outgoing text must fall below 30% opacity before incoming text becomes legible. There is no cursor; the play-button thumb moves only according to the timeline and pulses once from 1.0→1.06→1.0 when the route begins. The animation is silent, and all timings, springs, blur peaks and copy must live in timeline.config.js. Implement one pure seek(t) function that deterministically sets every visual property; ban Math.random, Date, requestAnimationFrame, setTimeout, CSS transitions and CSS animations; evaluate springs in closed form; bake the camera into a lookup table at a fixed 1/240s step; use 3D transforms only for map-card tilt while keeping text on flat layers; animate layout dimensions instead of scaling text; implement Face ID dot-to-stroke morphs with SVG paths and stroke-dasharray; and use SVG for every icon and flag with no emoji. For rendering, capture with Playwright at deviceScaleFactor: 2, downscale to 1080×1080, use four subframes per frame over a 180° shutter blended with FFmpeg tmix, pipe frames through image2pipe, and encode H.264, yuv420p, CRF 14, +faststart to out/wallet-morph.mp4 at 60fps. Validate stills at 0.0, 0.35, 0.7, 1.2, 1.6, 1.95, 2.3, 2.7, 3.1, 3.7, 4.2, 4.6, 5.0, 5.6, 6.1, 6.6 and 7.0 seconds, ensuring adjacent frames share geometry or blur-through with no hard cuts, Face ID dots and strokes align correctly, stack peeks remain even and the credit card is visible, the graticule-to-map transition and flatten are smooth, labels never collide or clip, the stats panel remains inside the map card, text never overlaps during swaps, and t=0.0 and t=7.0 are pixel-identical. Fix all failures before the final render. Do not use whole-scene fades as substitutes for morphs, dark mode, device chrome, player controls, emoji, backdrop-filter, network requests, random state, counting-up statistics, or any colors/effects outside the specified system. Deliver the complete project folder containing index.html, timeline.config.js, scripts/render.mjs, build script, out/wallet-morph.mp4 at 1080×1080/60fps/7.0s, out/stills/ containing all validation frames, and a concise README explaining how to change copy, cities, numbers and timings.

## Gap resolutions (decided by the implementer; all live in timeline.config.js so they can be changed)

The brief fully times S4–S6 but leaves S1–S3 implicit. Use these defaults, which line up with the validation stills:

| Segment | Time (s) | Morph |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | 0.00–1.00 | Tiny blurred squircle (≈120px, blur ≈20px, rotation −12°, opacity ≈0.55, wide soft-gray halo) emerges with the emergence spring (ω15 ζ0.72) into the 340px Wallet icon; rotation settles to 0°, halo tightens. Inner squircle / pocket / coral scallop / card edges lead by 80–150ms; “Wallet” label pops with the label spring (ω26 ζ0.70) after the shape. Hold until ≈1.00. |
| S2 | 1.00–2.20 | Icon blurs (peak 14–24px) and condenses into glowing #1E90FF dots placed at the start points of the Face ID strokes (1.00–1.35); the dots stretch into the strokes via stroke-dasharray along the SAME SVG paths (1.35–1.85, 30–50ms stagger: brackets, eyes, nose, smile); two glow layers; short hold to 2.20. |
| S3 | 2.20–3.80 | Face ID strokes retract and the glyph's bounding geometry becomes a small black card (≈220×140, blur-through) (2.20–2.45); the card grows with the card-growth spring (ω16 ζ0.82) into the tall Wallet card (84% width = 907px, radius 44, cropped below the canvas bottom) (2.45–3.05); three stack cards (#489CCB, #5CC24A, #F07566) peek evenly above it; content enters after the shape, staggered 30–50ms: 56px “Wallet” title, 68px white “+” button, money-bag SVG, balance “<currency> 120,000” revealed digit-by-digit (each digit rises/unblurs in sequence, not a numeric count-up) (≈2.95–3.40), progress bar animates 48% → 80% (#22E83C) (≈3.15–3.70), and a credit card (visible, using stack palette) sits in the lower part of the card. |
| S4 | 3.80–5.00 | As in the brief. |
| S5 | 5.00–6.30 | As in the brief. Camera push is applied to the map content only (map SVG transform from the baked camera LUT); labels/bubbles live on flat layers positioned by projecting city coordinates through the same LUT, so text is never scaled. |
| S6 | 6.30–7.00 | As in the brief; exits ≈40% faster; ends exactly at the t=0 state. |

Other defaults:
- **Currency symbol**: the brief's balance copy “ 120,000” arrived with its leading symbol missing. Use `$` in config (`copy.currency`) and note it in the README so the animator can swap it (e.g. `₹`). Do not use Apple's private-use logo glyph.
- **Stats panel copy** (static, never counting): `Time 3h 05m`, `Flight 185 min`, `Distance 1,930 km`, with a slider track + thumb and a 0A84FF round play button; glass fill rgba(70,70,70,0.55) with white text. No backdrop-filter.
- **Bubbles**: “India” bubble anchored at Mumbai with the #FFC83D origin ring; “Dubai” bubble anchored in the UAE, UAE fill #EF5B55.
- **Wallet icon neutrals** (light-gray outer, dark-gray inner, cream pocket) are not in the palette list; use restrained neutrals (e.g. #E9E9EB, #2B2B2D, #F4EBD9) and the stack coral #F07566 for the scalloped edge. Keep them in config.
- **Halo**: soft gray only (rgba of black at low alpha), wide while floating, tighter when settled. No other effects.
- **Loop**: t=7.0 must render pixel-identical to t=0.0 (seek(t) should evaluate t mod 7 so 7.0 ≡ 0.0, and the S6 end state must equal the S1 start state by construction, not by a cut).
